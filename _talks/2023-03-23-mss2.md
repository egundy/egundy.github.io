Panel presentation alongside Dr. Jason Torkelson discussing early findings from our research on ideological effects of perceived decline among white Americans. Information in this presentation was further developed into our publication: *The Racialized American Dream: Predictors for the 21st Century*. We employed logistic regression on nationally representative survey data that revealed how various racial groups within the United States interact with the national mythos of the American Dream.

The full paper can be found at **[The Sociological Quarterly](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/00380253.2024.2387359)**.
