Panel presentation alongside Dr. Neeraj Rajasekar at the 86th Annual Midwestern Sociological Society Conference. We presented early findings of our paper: *The language of “diversity” or “DEI”? Exploring job titles of diversity professionals in US institutions of higher education*. This project synthesized multiple datasets sourced from both government (Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System) and private organizations (Higher Education Publications) to empirically demonstrate a discursive transition within institutions of higher education--changing language from "Diversity" to "Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion." We further employed multiple regression techniques to investigate the linkages between institutional characteristics, and the likelihood of adopting new discourses.

The full, open-access paper can be found at **[The Sociological Forum](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/socf.13047)**.
